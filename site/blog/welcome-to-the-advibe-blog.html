Why we're starting a blog (and what it won't be)

A short note on why this exists, who it's for, and the one promise we're making before we publish anything else here.

Most agency blogs exist to rank for keywords, not to say anything. You know the type — a thousand words that could have been written by anyone, about anything, for any market. We're not interested in adding to that pile.

This blog is where we write down what's actually happening inside our own ad accounts and our clients' — the campaign that underperformed for two weeks before we found the audience overlap killing it, the landing page change that moved conversion rate more than any amount of extra budget would have, the thing about the Dubai market specifically that a generic playbook gets wrong. If it's here, it's because we learned it running real spend, not because it was easy to write.

What you can expect

Notes on Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, and the rest of what we do day to day — written for the UAE market specifically, with real numbers where we can share them and an honest "we don't know yet" where we can't. Some posts will be short. Some will be one chart and three sentences. None of them will be written to hit a word count.

We treat your ad spend like data, not vibes — this blog is that same rule applied to what we publish.

The one promise

If a post here turns out to be wrong — a tactic stops working, a platform changes something, we were just off — we'll update it and say so, not quietly delete it. Marketing writing has a habit of only ever being right in hindsight. We'd rather be useful in real time.

If there's something specific about Google Ads, Meta Ads, or SEO in the UAE you'd want us to write about, tell us — the next post is more likely to be an answer to that than anything we'd have picked ourselves.

Abdul Rahim Edavazhikkal Founder & Digital Growth Partner, Advibe Agency
