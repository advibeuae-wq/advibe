Why TikTok now

Still an underused channel for UAE brands

Most performance budgets in the region are still split between Google and Meta. That gap is exactly why TikTok is worth testing while it's less crowded.

A younger, highly engaged audience

TikTok's user base skews younger than Facebook and Instagram, and the app is built around long, active watch sessions — attention that's harder to buy on more saturated platforms.

Native creative outperforms polished ads

The format rewards video that looks like it belongs in the feed, not a traditional ad — which usually means lower production costs than a Meta or YouTube campaign.

Less competition, more efficient reach

Fewer UAE brands are running TikTok Ads than Google or Meta, which for now often means a more efficient cost-per-result for advertisers willing to test it.
